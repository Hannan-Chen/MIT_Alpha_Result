# Source access and attribution

Original source datasets are not redistributed here.

- MIT Supercloud dataset: https://registry.opendata.aws/dcc/
- Public data bucket: s3://mit-supercloud-dataset/ (AWS us-west-2).
- Samsi et al., The MIT Supercloud Dataset, IEEE HPEC 2021; https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.02037
- Source license: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/
- Weather source: Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api
- Weather attribution: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com. CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Alpha paper: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2024.125100
- Alpha implementation: https://github.com/wfzheng/AlphaDataCenterCooling ; commit 1f4a704cd8ff8c3f2679fd88b1d30bdec04275d2.

This repository contains generated cooling-model results and their English documentation. The original GPU/weather packages, source-only tables, third-party FMU binary, serialized model weights, and publisher paper PDF are not attached. Rescaled model-load requests and implemented water/weather boundaries remain in simulation records to document their generation. Refer to upstream terms before obtaining or reusing source material; this repository does not replace those terms or grant a blanket license to third-party artifacts.
